Autopost Helper — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Autopost Helper is a personal tool operated by its owner to manage the owner's own Threads account. It is not offered to the public, and it is used only by the Threads account that has authorized it.

What data we access

  • Your Threads profile basics (user ID and username), via the Threads API, to identify the connected account.
  • Your own posts and replies (text and timestamps), to learn the writing style of the connected account.
  • Public Threads posts found by keyword search (text, author username, link, publication time, and public engagement counts), to pick topics and posts the connected account may reply to.
  • An access token issued by Threads, used to make API calls on behalf of the connected account.

How we use it

  • To draft and publish posts and replies on the connected Threads account.
  • To avoid repeating content and to respect posting limits.

We do not sell, rent, or share data for advertising. We do not build profiles of other Threads users.

Third-party processors

  • Google Gemini API — post texts are sent to generate drafts.
  • Apify — used to find public Threads posts by keyword.

These services process data only to perform those functions, under their own terms.

Storage and retention

Data is stored locally on the operator's own computer and is not uploaded to any server we run. Fetched public posts are deleted automatically after 30 days. The access token is kept until it expires or is revoked.

Deleting your data

You can revoke access at any time in the Threads app: Settings → Account → Website permissions → Active → Autopost Helper → Remove. After removal, the operator deletes all locally stored data related to your account within 30 days. You can also request deletion by email at m.music11111@gmail.com.
